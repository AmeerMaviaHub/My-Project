A corner shop, moved online.

Nukkad is the Hindi word for the corner of a street, and that's the kind of shop we wanted to run: a small counter, a short list of things we'd happily use ourselves, and a shopkeeper who answers when you ask a question.

We began with a handful of watches and a few pairs of shoes. Every product on the shelf is something we've handled, worn or carried for a few weeks before listing it. If it didn't hold up, it didn't make the list.

Prices are kept honest, delivery is a flat fee that disappears above ₹1,999, and returns are open for seven days.
